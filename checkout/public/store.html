How buying works

No account and no card. Checkout runs in a Telegram chat and payment is a plain USDC transfer.

  1. Tap BuyThis opens our shop bot on Telegram with the product picked.
  2. Pick Base or SolanaThe bot gives you an exact USDC amount (with a few unique cents) and one address to send it to.
  3. Send that exact amountSend it from any wallet or exchange. The cents are how we match your payment to your order.
  4. Get your keyIt arrives in the same chat within about {{CHECK_EVERY_MIN}} minutes, with setup steps. Type /status there any time to see it again.

    Trust and safety

    Read this before you connect anything to real money.

    We never hold your funds or keysThe bots run on your computer or your VPS. Your wallet key goes in a file there and is never sent to us.
    Paper mode by defaultA new bot trades with simulated money until you switch it to live yourself. Watch it first.
    Simulated before sentEvery live transaction is dry-run against the chain first. If the simulation fails, nothing is sent.
    Guard railsStop loss and take profit are in the free version, because safety is never paywalled. The DLMM Agent cuts at −15% by default. Before the autopilot opens a pool, it checks the token contract for mint, blacklist and tax powers.
    The dashboard is read-onlyIt only needs your public address and never asks for a signature. Addresses and history stay in your browser.
    Open codeThe free versions are public, so you can read exactly what runs before you pay for anything.

    FAQ

    Which chains and protocols are supported?
    LP Bot: Uniswap v3 on Robinhood Chain, Base and Arbitrum, and Uniswap v4 on Robinhood Chain. DLMM Agent: Meteora DLMM on Solana. Dashboard: all of the above in one view.
    Is the dashboard really free?
    Yes, for one wallet with live value, unclaimed fees and range status. Pro adds up to 20 wallets, a daily history of value and fees with charts, CSV export, auto-refresh, and Telegram alerts when a position goes out of range. Type /trial in the shop bot to try Pro free for 7 days, no payment needed.
    What do I need to run a bot?
    A Telegram account, a wallet you use only for the bot, and a computer or a cheap VPS that stays on. The setup option means we install and harden it on your VPS for you, so your keys still stay on your server.
    I sent the wrong amount, or my key hasn't arrived
    Payments are checked every few minutes. Type /status in the shop chat first. If you sent a different amount, or the key still hasn't come after 30 minutes, message us with your order id and the transaction link.
    How does the monthly dashboard plan renew?
    It doesn't renew automatically, because nothing is ever charged without you sending it. The shop bot reminds you 3 days before your key runs out. If you renew early, the new key adds 30 days on top of what you have left. You can also choose lifetime and never think about it again.
    Can a bot lose money?
    Yes. Providing liquidity can lose money, especially on volatile tokens: the price can move against your range, and past fees don't predict future ones. The bots manage positions, they don't guarantee profit. Start in paper mode, then go live with an amount you can afford to lose.
    Why Telegram?
    It's where the bots are controlled from, and it lets checkout work without accounts, cards or a server holding your data. All we see is your Telegram chat and the payment.

    This is software, not an investment product or advice. You run it, you hold the keys, and you decide what it trades. Providing liquidity carries risk, including total loss.
