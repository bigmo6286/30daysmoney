Get a Telegram alert when your Uniswap position goes out of range

When the price leaves your range, your position stops earning and sits entirely in one token. If you don't notice for a day, that's a day of fees lost. Here's how to get a message as soon as it happens, without running anything yourself.

Set it up

  1. Open the shop bot from the store and send /trial. That gives you Dashboard Pro free for 7 days, with no payment.
  2. Send /watch 0x… with your wallet address. You can watch up to 5 wallets.
  3. The first check records where your positions are. After that you get a message when one leaves its range ("⚠️ Out of range: …") and when it's back ("✅ Back in range").

Wallets are checked about every 15 minutes. The alerts cover Uniswap v3 on Robinhood Chain, Base and Arbitrum, and Uniswap v4 on Robinhood Chain. A wallet whose read fails is skipped rather than reported as out of range, so a flaky RPC doesn't cause false alarms.

What else Pro includes

Up to 20 wallets in the dashboard, daily history charts of value and fees, CSV export and auto-refresh. After the trial it's $15 for 30 days, or $39 for lifetime.
