A Telegram LP bot for Robinhood Chain

LP Bot manages Uniswap liquidity on Robinhood Chain (and Base and Arbitrum) from a Telegram chat: paste a token, pick a pool, choose an amount, confirm. It runs on your machine with your key. Nobody else holds your funds.

What it does

  • Uniswap v3 and v4. On Robinhood Chain, v4 often has more trading than v3. The bot lists both side by side, and only uses v4 pools without hooks.
  • Paper mode by default. It reads real pools, quotes real swaps and estimates fees, but sends nothing until you switch it to live.
  • Simulated before sent. Every live transaction is dry-run first. Approvals are for exact amounts, never unlimited.
  • Monitoring. Out-of-range alerts, take-profit, stop loss, and auto-rebalance after a grace period.
  • Autopilot (Pro). It scans pools, screens tokens (contract checks plus a buy→sell round trip and the Jev rug/copycat screen), opens positions and rotates out of them.
