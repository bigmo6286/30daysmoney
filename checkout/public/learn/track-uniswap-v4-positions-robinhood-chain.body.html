How to track Uniswap v4 LP positions on Robinhood Chain

Uniswap v4 positions are harder to find than v3 ones. The v4 PositionManager can't list the NFTs a wallet owns, and fees are held in the PoolManager rather than in the position. Here's how to see all of them, with value, unclaimed fees and range, in one place.

Open the free dashboard

The quick way

  1. Open the LP PnL dashboard. It runs in your browser, with no account and no wallet connection.
  2. Paste the wallet address (0x…).
  3. The dashboard reads Uniswap v3 on Robinhood Chain, Base and Arbitrum, and Uniswap v4 on Robinhood Chain. Each open position shows its value, unclaimed fees, and whether it's in range.

It only reads from the chain. It never asks for a signature and can't move anything.

Why v4 positions are harder to find

The dashboard does all of this for you. On Robinhood Chain the public RPC accepts log queries over large block ranges, so scanning a wallet's history takes seconds. Free tiers of some RPC providers cap log queries at 10 blocks, which makes the same scan impractical.

Out-of-range alerts

A position out of range earns nothing. Dashboard Pro adds Telegram alerts: send /watch 0x… to the shop bot and you get a message when a position leaves its range or comes back. You can try Pro free for 7 days.

The dashboard is open source (pnl-dashboard/ in the repo). The v4 reader is src/sources/univ4.ts. It was checked against live Robinhood Chain positions, for example a GLD/USDG v4 position worth about $10,187 with $42.43 in unclaimed fees.
